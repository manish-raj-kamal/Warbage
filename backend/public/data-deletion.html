7-Day Self-Service Deletion Window

Warbage provides users with complete autonomy over their data. When you initiate account deletion from the app, you enter a 7-day self-service window during which you may cancel the request if initiated by mistake, or allow the system to permanently and irreversibly erase all personal records at the conclusion of the 7-day period.

1 Self-Service In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You do not need to contact customer support or wait for administrative approval to delete your data. You can initiate your account deletion directly inside the Android application at any time:

1

Open Profile Tab

Launch the Warbage app on your Android device and tap the Profile tab in the bottom liquid navigation bar.

2

Request Account Deletion (7-Day Period)

Scroll down to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap "Request Account Deletion (7-Day Period)". Confirm your decision on the confirmation dialog.

3

7-Day Grace & Recovery Window

Your profile immediately displays a visible countdown banner stating that your account is scheduled for deletion in 7 days. During these 7 days, your open lots and active pickup requests are paused to prevent new transactions.

4

Permanent Automated Purge

Once the 7-day period expires, our backend service and Room database permanently purge your user profile, personal information, authentication credentials, and open records. You will be automatically signed out and cannot recover the deleted account.

Made a Mistake? If you change your mind within the 7-day period, simply log in to your Warbage app, open the Profile screen, and tap "Cancel Account Deletion" to instantly restore your account to active standing.

2 Manual Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application, lost your device, or cannot log in to your account, you can submit an offline deletion request directly to our developer team:

  • Email Address: m18labs.dev@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - com.warbage.recycling
  • Required Information:
    1. Your registered email address associated with your Warbage account.
    2. Your registered phone number (used to verify ownership).
    3. Your user role (Collector or Recycler).
  • Turnaround Time: Our developer team processes identity verification and initiates the permanent purge within 7 business days. Once finalized, a confirmation notice will be sent to your email.

3 Data Retention & Permanent Deletion Matrix

Under Google Play Data Safety policies, GDPR, and Indian DPDP guidelines, we clearly disclose what records are wiped versus what is retained:

Data Type Status Upon Deletion Details & Timeline
Personal Identity Permanently Deleted Your Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Firebase UID, and profile role are permanently erased from MongoDB and Firebase Authentication within 7 days.
Open Scrap Lots Permanently Deleted All active or open e-waste lots created by your account are removed from public and recycler listings.
Pending Pickup Requests Cancelled & Purged Any pending scrap pickup requests are immediately marked as REJECTED/cancelled and purged to release counterpart obligations.
On-Device Local Cache Purged Room database records on your Android device are cleared upon sign out or uninstallation.
Past Completed Transactions Anonymized Record Past settled scrap transactions involving other certified recyclers retain numerical weight and settlement totals for mandatory financial accounting and tax audits; however, all personal names, phone numbers, and identifying collector details are stripped and irreversibly anonymized.
