1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Warbage, a smart electronic waste (e-waste) aggregation and recycling platform developed and operated by Warbage Eco Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to maintaining the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal data entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Warbage mobile application (Package Name: com.warbage.recycling) available on the Google Play Store, along with our supporting backend cloud infrastructure hosted at https://warbage.vercel.app.

By downloading, installing, accessing, or using the Warbage app, you consent to the collection, processing, and handling of information described in this Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue using the service.

2 Information We Collect

We strictly limit the data we collect to information that is technically and operationally essential for connecting waste collectors with authorized recyclers:

Data Category Specific Data Items Primary Purpose
Account & Identity Full Name, Email address, Phone number, Profile role (COLLECTOR or RECYCLER), Firebase UID. Authentication, profile verification, role authorization, and account security.
E-Waste Lots Material category (e.g., Battery, PCB, Keyboard, Mobile), weight in kilograms, estimated scrap price, lot status. Inventory management, transparent pricing calculation, and bilateral trade matching.
Trade Requests & Transactions Collector contact details, Recycler facility name, proposed pickup date, status, notes, settlement amount. Facilitating physical pickup, inspection, verification, and transaction history.
Technical Metadata Firebase session tokens, app version code, crash logs. Session management, bug fixing, service stability, and API authorization.
Financial & Payment Cards: Warbage does NOT store or process bank account numbers, credit/debit card numbers, or CVVs. All scrap settlement valuations displayed in the application serve as operational guides for bilateral trade execution.

3 Device Permissions & Camera Policy

To maintain transparent adherence to the Google Play Developer Distribution Policy, the following device capabilities are requested only when necessary:

Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

  • Purpose: The camera is used solely to enable real-time visual AI scanning of e-waste scrap items (such as identifying Printed Circuit Boards, Batteries, Mobile Devices, Appliances, and peripherals) and attaching optional lot inspection photos.
  • On-Device Machine Learning: Image classification is executed locally on your mobile device using TensorFlow Lite / LiteRT neural network inference models.
  • No Passive or Background Streaming: The camera feed is never accessed in the background, is never monitored passively, and raw camera frames are discarded immediately after local on-device inference completes.

Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

  • Purpose: Allows the application to securely synchronize catalog prices, scrap lots, and requests with our encrypted cloud backend, as well as verify your authentication session.

4 Location Data Policy (Zero GPS Tracking)

Zero Location Tracking Guarantee: Warbage does NOT request, collect, monitor, track, or share your device's GPS location, coarse background location, or fine foreground location. All location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) have been completely removed from our production manifests.

Any geographical information visible in the app (such as a recycler's operational city, drop-off hub address, or pickup landmark) is manually typed and submitted by the registered user in their profile or trade request forms for operational coordination.

5 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide, maintain, and protect the recycling platform, specifically:

  1. User Authentication: Verifying your identity via Firebase Email Verification or Google Sign-In, and preventing unauthorized access.
  2. Bilateral Scrap Exchange: Enabling collectors to publish available scrap quantities and enabling certified recyclers to review, accept, and schedule pickups.
  3. Pricing Accuracy: Providing transparent market baseline rates for distinct electronic waste categories.
  4. Account Management & Compliance: Responding to user support inquiries, processing account deletion requests, and fulfilling legal obligations.

6 Bilateral Data Sharing & Disclosure

Warbage is a bilateral platform. In order for collectors and recyclers to physically transact electronic waste, certain operational contact data must be mutually visible between the transacting parties:

  • When a Collector creates an offer with a Recycler: The designated Recycler can view the Collector's name, registered phone number, scrap lot contents, and operational notes to coordinate pickup.
  • When a Collector views available Recyclers: The Collector can view the Recycler's name, contact information, offered price per kg, and drop-off/pickup location details.
No Third-Party Commercial Sale: We strictly do NOT sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing intermediaries. Data is accessed solely by the parties participating in the specific recycling transaction.

8 Data Security & Storage Retention

We employ state-of-the-art administrative, physical, and electronic security safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Warbage mobile client and backend servers are enforced over HTTPS utilizing TLS 1.2/1.3 with SHA-256 encryption.
  • Authentication Tokens: API endpoints require cryptographically signed RS256 JSON Web Tokens (JWT) verified against Google Firebase public certificates.
  • Data Isolation: Database access queries enforce strict tenancy filters, ensuring non-participating accounts cannot query or access private trade records.
  • Retention Policy: Active account data is retained for the lifetime of your active profile to maintain your transaction and lot history. If you delete your account, your data is expunged according to the protocol below.

9 Account & Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements and global privacy regulations, all users have the absolute right to permanently delete their account and associated personal information at any time.

Method 1: Immediate Self-Service In-App Deletion

You can delete your account directly inside the Android application in just three steps:

1
Open the Warbage app and tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
2
Scroll to the bottom of the Profile screen and tap Delete Account.
3
Confirm the deletion prompt. The app immediately calls our secured DELETE /api/users/:id API, permanently wiping your user record, closing open scrap lots, and invalidating your login credentials.

Method 2: Email & Web Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the mobile application or wish to submit an offline erasure request:

  • Send an email to m18labs.dev@gmail.com.
  • Use the subject line: Account and Data Deletion Request - Warbage.
  • Provide your registered email address and phone number for ownership verification.
  • Upon verification, our administrative team will permanently purge your user profile, personal identifiers, open lots, and associated metadata from our database within 30 days, and confirm completion via email.
7-Day Self-Service Deletion Window: When you initiate account deletion from the app, you enter a 7-day self-service window. During these 7 days, you can log in anytime to cancel the deletion request. After 7 days, the account is permanently purged. For full details and manual requests, see our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Page.

10 Children's Privacy

Warbage is designed strictly for adult collectors, commercial recyclers, and individuals of legal working age (18 years or older, or at least 13 with verified parental guidance where allowed by law). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13.

If we discover that personal data has been inadvertently collected from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will immediately take steps to permanently delete that information from our records. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately at m18labs.dev@gmail.com.

11 Your Rights & Legal Protections

Depending on your location, you may possess specific rights under data protection laws (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act):

  • Right to Access: The right to request copies of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete profile information directly via the app.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): The right to request the permanent deletion of your personal records.
  • Right to Data Portability: The right to receive your transaction history in a structured, commonly used format.
